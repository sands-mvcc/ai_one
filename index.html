About Professor John Sands

Throughout my tenure at Moraine Valley Community College, I have prioritized a teaching model built around applied laboratory scenarios rather than rigid memorization. I believe learning functions best when it feels active, transparent, and engaging.

My background straddles academic research and operational enterprise security. This dual context allows me to adapt quickly to emerging paradigms—integrating practical security models, cloud automation pathways, and applied mathematical metrics seamlessly into academic programming templates.
