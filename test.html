Question 1 10 Points

Which city is the capital of California?

Explanation: Sacramento became California's permanent state capital in 1854 during the famous California Gold Rush era.
Question 2 10 Points

What is the capital city of Texas?

Explanation: Austin is the capital of Texas and houses the beautiful Texas State Capitol building, which is made of unique pink granite.
Question 3 10 Points

Albany is the capital city of which northeastern state?

Explanation: Albany has been the capital city of New York State since 1797 and is located right along the historic Hudson River.
Question 4 10 Points

If you visit the city of Tallahassee, which state's capitol building are you touring?

Explanation: Tallahassee was selected as Florida's territory capital in 1824 because it sat right between the major historic population centers of St. Augustine and Pensacola.
Question 5 10 Points

Which of these midwestern cities serves as the official capital of Illinois?

Explanation: Springfield is the capital of Illinois and is famous for being the home of President Abraham Lincoln, who lived and worked there for many years.
Question 6 (Matching) 10 Points

Match each state on the left to its correct capital city from the choices.

1. Washington State
2. Oregon
3. Arizona
Explanation: Washington is paired with Olympia; Oregon is paired with Salem; and Arizona is paired with Phoenix.
Question 7 (Matching) 10 Points

Match each state on the left to its correct capital city from the choices.

1. Georgia
2. Tennessee
3. Ohio
Explanation: Georgia is paired with Atlanta; Tennessee is paired with Nashville; and Ohio is paired with Columbus.
Question 8 10 Points

Denver is the capital city of the state of _______________.

Explanation: Denver, widely known as the "Mile High City" because it rests exactly one mile above sea level, is the official capital of Colorado.
Question 9 (Short Essay) 10 Points

Honolulu is the capital city of Hawaii. Describe what makes Hawaii's geographic location unique compared to all other U.S. state capitals.

Teacher Evaluation Rubric: Full credit (10 points) is awarded if the student states that Honolulu is located on an island chain out in the Pacific Ocean, making it the only tropical island state capital in the United States.
Question 10 (Short Essay) 10 Points

Why do you think states choose to build their capital cities near the center of the state, or near large rivers? Explain your reasoning using an example you have learned.

Teacher Evaluation Rubric: Full credit (10 points) is awarded if the student explains that rivers provided vital transportation, trade, and drinking water during early settlement, or that a central location made it easier for people all over the state to travel to the capital.
